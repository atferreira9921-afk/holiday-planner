================================================================================
  HOLIDAY PLANNER — PITCH & DEMO SCRIPT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE HOOK (30 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Planning a group holiday is chaos.

You're in 4 different WhatsApp threads trying to find a week when everyone is
free. Someone forgets they have a conference. Someone else used up their vacation
days. You spend more time coordinating than actually planning.

Then once you finally pick dates, you still have to figure out where to go,
find flights, compare hotels, split costs, make packing lists, and share all the
documents — across 6 different apps.

Holiday Planner fixes all of that. One app. Everyone in sync. AI doing the
heavy lifting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE PROBLEM (1 minute)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Group travel has three stages of pain:

1. FINDING THE WINDOW
   Everyone has different calendars, vacation day budgets, public holidays,
   and family commitments. Finding even a 5-day overlap where everyone can go
   is a spreadsheet nightmare.

2. DECIDING WHERE TO GO
   Once you have dates, someone has to research destinations that fit everyone's
   budget, interests, and travel style — including who's flying from where.

3. MANAGING THE TRIP
   After the decision: flights, hotels, expenses, packing, documents, itineraries,
   polls, notes — scattered across Google Docs, Splitwise, WhatsApp, and email.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE SOLUTION (1 minute)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Holiday Planner is an AI-powered multi-user travel coordination platform.

It connects everyone's calendars, automatically surfaces windows when the whole
group is free, then uses Claude (Anthropic's AI) to generate ranked destination
suggestions — with real flight prices, hotel estimates, car rental guidance, and
a full reasoning explaining why each destination fits this specific group.

Once you pick a destination, the app becomes your shared trip workspace:
expenses, packing, itinerary, documents, polls, notes — all in one place,
live-synced for every member.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  KEY DIFFERENTIATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * GROUP-FIRST, not solo-first. Calendars, preferences, and budgets for
    every member are considered together. The AI knows Alice has 8 vacation
    days left, Bob is flying from London, and Clara has a public holiday on
    the 15th.

  * AI THAT KNOWS YOUR CONSTRAINTS. Not just "suggest a destination" —
    the AI receives everyone's blocked dates, interests, budget ranges,
    travel styles, public holidays, and even free stays (friends/family with
    spare rooms), then returns 3 ranked options with trade-off analysis.

  * BRIDGE-DAY INTELLIGENCE. The app finds "bridge days" — where booking
    just 1-2 extra vacation days creates a 9-10 day run. Most people leave
    these on the table. Holiday Planner surfaces them automatically.

  * EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE. From "when can we go?" to shared expense
    splitting and a printable trip summary — one app, not six.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DEMO FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Suggested order for a live demo (approx 10-15 minutes):

--- STEP 1: Dashboard (1 min) ---
  - Show the vacation day counter (days used / remaining)
  - Point out the bridge-day cards — "3 days booked = 9 days off"
  - Show the 3D globe with wishlist pins
  - Mention: "This is everyone's state at a glance"

--- STEP 2: Holiday Calendar (2 min) ---
  - Show a multi-month calendar with colour-coded members
  - Book a holiday period for one member — watch the calendar update live
  - Show public holidays auto-populated for Portugal, UK, etc.
  - Highlight the Bridge-Day Optimizer panel on the right

--- STEP 3: Create a Trip (1 min) ---
  - Click "New Trip" → choose AI-suggest mode
  - Set a date window (e.g. July 15 – August 10), 7 days, €800/person budget
  - Add a custom prompt: "We want beaches, somewhere we haven't been before"

--- STEP 4: AI Suggestion Generation (3 min) ---
  - Click "Generate Suggestions" — watch the progress bar stream live
  - Show the 3 suggestions arrive: destination, dates, costs, reasoning,
    highlights, trade-offs
  - Point out: "It factored in both members' calendars and public holidays"
  - Show the car rental panel for a destination that needs one
    ("Rural Alentejo — public transport is limited, car recommended, ~€180")
  - Show the Hotel Search panel — live Google Hotels results
  - Show the Flight Search panel — live Google Flights results
  - Group members vote up/down on suggestions

--- STEP 5: Wishlist & Free Stays (1 min) ---
  - Show a wishlist destination on the 3D globe
  - Show a Free Stay entry: "Friend's apartment in Barcelona"
  - Toggle it active/inactive — explain: "Inactive ones are excluded from AI"

--- STEP 6: Trip Detail — Shared Workspace (2 min) ---
  - Scroll through the trip detail tabs: Expenses, Packing, Itinerary,
    Documents, Photos, Notes, Polls
  - Add an expense: "Flights — €320" — split equally
  - Check off a packing item
  - Show the Local Info card (visa, currency, timezone, language, safety)
  - Show the Currency widget (live exchange rate)

--- STEP 7: Notifications (30 sec) ---
  - Show the notification bell — "Suggestions ready for Summer Trip 2025"
  - Mention: "Every group member gets notified — no one misses an update"

--- STEP 8: Profile & Preferences (1 min) ---
  - Show travel preferences: travel style, budget, interests, avoided countries
  - Show parental leave toggle (blocks calendar days automatically)
  - Show My Cars with AI fuel consumption estimator
  - Mention Facebook login as an option

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Q&A PREP — LIKELY QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How does the AI know about flight prices?
A: Real-time search via SerpApi (Google Flights / Google Hotels), called
   live when you open the suggestion panel.

Q: What if two people are in different countries?
A: Each member has their own home country, airport, and public holiday
   calendar. The AI gets everyone's data and suggests departure dates that
   work for all of them.

Q: Can you use it solo?
A: Yes — create a group with just yourself. It still tracks your calendar,
   vacation days, and generates personal suggestions.

Q: Is the data private?
A: All data is stored in Supabase with row-level security. You only see your
   own data and trips you've been invited to.

Q: Can you add family members who don't have an account?
A: Yes — add them as profiles (child, partner, etc.) and link them to an
   app account later if they join.

Q: Why not just use Google Trips / TripIt?
A: Those are for managing trips you've already booked. Holiday Planner is
   for the coordination phase before booking — finding the window, picking
   the destination, building group consensus.
